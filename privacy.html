Privacy Policy

Last updated:

ZISHU TRON ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and safeguard your information when you use our website, applications and services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us and information that is automatically collected when you use our services.

Information You Provide

  • Account information: When you create a ZISHU TRON account, we collect your email address, display name and profile picture (if provided through a sign-in provider).
  • Authentication data: We use Firebase Authentication to manage sign-ins. We do not store your passwords directly.
  • Communications: If you contact us, we may keep a record of that correspondence.

Information Automatically Collected

  • Login activity: We record sign-in timestamps, device type and platform information to help you manage your account security.
  • Usage data: Basic technical information such as browser type and device capabilities.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your ZISHU TRON account across all our products and services.
  • To authenticate you and provide access to your account.
  • To maintain account security and detect unauthorized access.
  • To improve our products and services.
  • To respond to your enquiries and provide support.

3. One Account Across ZISHU TRON Products

A single ZISHU TRON account can be used across all our products and services. When you sign in to a ZISHU TRON product, your account details are shared securely through Firebase Authentication. Each product may access basic profile information (email, display name) to personalize your experience.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Firebase Realtime Database and Firebase Authentication, provided by Google. We implement reasonable security measures, including:

  • Encrypted authentication (managed by Firebase).
  • Database security rules that restrict access to your own data.
  • No storage of plaintext passwords.

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share limited information only with:

  • Service providers: Firebase (Google) for authentication and database services.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights.

6. Your Rights

You have control over your account information:

  • Access: View your account details from the My Account page.
  • Update: Change your display name and profile picture at any time.
  • Delete: Permanently delete your account and associated data from the My Account page.
  • Login Activity: Review your recent sign-in activity from the My Account page.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser local storage to remember your theme preference (light or dark mode). We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us.
